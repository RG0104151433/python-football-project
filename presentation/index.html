04 / FROM RECORDS TO A PREDICTION

Predict first. Learn from the result afterwards.

Both opponents contribute information. A home result is interpreted in light of the venue and the opponent.

earlier results → uncertain strength → probability

The joint model receives a full strength-difference distribution. It estimates context separately; the rating model's home term is excluded from that input.

This is a workflow animation, not a live model refit. Friendly results carry less information; repeated surprising results can support a lasting change, without a guaranteed detection speed.

10 / WHAT THIS STUDY CONTRIBUTES

From historical patterns to a prediction we can inspect.

01 / UNDERSTAND THE DATA

Rates depend on who played.

Compare compositions before interpreting differences across time, match types or regions.

02 / LEARN FROM HISTORY

Strength carries the larger increment.

Track changing ability and uncertainty, then test what context adds on the same matches.

03 / STATE THE LIMITS

Probability is more informative than a confident pick.

Keep the issued forecasts unchanged. Next: evaluate new outcomes, improve calibration, and extend the joint target to win–draw–loss.
