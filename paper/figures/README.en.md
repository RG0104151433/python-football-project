Check the actual inclusions in [results.tex](../language_revision_20261107/sections/results.tex) and [source guide, Chinese](../图表来源说明.md). `strength_validation_rows.tex` supplies generated table rows. ## Provenance and regeneration

Use [historical_overview_manifest.json](../historical_overview_manifest.json), [strength_evidence_manifest.json](../strength_evidence_manifest.json) and [results_evidence.json](../results_evidence.json) to trace adopted data and selected rows.

`paper/make_paper_figures.py` regenerates the older candidate set, not every final figure. `paper/make_historical_overview.py` and `paper/make_strength_evidence.py` serve their named outputs. Do not run them merely to read the report; generated bytes may change with fonts or libraries.

Prefer vector PDF for the report and PNG for previews. Captions must state sample, period, units, interpretation and limits. Do not label conditional model ranges as unconditional confidence guarantees or causal effects. The study diagram and final figure selection are complete; earlier “still to draw/select” notes are historical.
