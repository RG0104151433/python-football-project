# Original FIFA membership material

[中文](README.md) | **English**


`FIFA_Financial_Report_2018.pdf` was obtained from FIFA's digitalhub. PDF pages 149–150 list 211 associations and joining years, with an editorial cutoff of 31 December 2018. URL, SHA, extraction method and limits are registered in `data/external/team_identity/fifa/20261024-v2/sources.json` from the repository root.

`FIFA_2018_members_page_149.txt` and `FIFA_2018_members_page_150.txt` are layout-preserving pypdf 6.21.2 text extracts, not independent sources. Sequence numbers 1–211 and unique codes were checked. Production code reads the registered CSV and needs no PDF library. No image-level cell-by-cell human review is claimed.

Joining year is not founding year. Slash-separated re-entry years are preserved; exact dates are not invented. The 2018 list alone cannot establish membership throughout all historical periods, and the 2024 total cannot prove uninterrupted historical status. Copyright remains with the original rights holders; archival research use is not an open-source relicensing claim.
