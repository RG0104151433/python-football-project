# Question 9: outcome prediction, probability improvement and club transfer

[中文](README.md) | **English**


## Question and method

What capabilities remain missing, and how could testable improvements extend the method? Starting from Questions 1–8, discuss four areas: a complete three-way joint model, probability calibration, better information and mechanisms, and differences between national-team and club football with a minimal transfer design.

## Findings and limits

The four analyses and their joint conclusion are complete. They provide candidate constructions, mathematical reasoning, applicability limits and future evaluation principles. No new optimization experiment establishes a performance gain.

The underlying rating model already has three-way outcomes; the proposed extension concerns the joint prediction layer. Club-transfer design belongs to this question, while actual club-data acquisition and experiments remain an optional Question 10. A valid formula alone does not demonstrate better forecasts.
